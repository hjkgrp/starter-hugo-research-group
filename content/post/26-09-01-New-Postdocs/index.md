Welcome to a host of new arrivals in the group:
- Dr. Shams Mehdi joins us as a postdoc from the Isayaev lab at CMU and a PhD with Pratyush Tiwary.
- Dr. Ignacio Migliaro joins us a postdoc from Stirling lab at UT Dallas and a PhD with Thomas Cundari.

- Dr. Jason Yang joins us as a Tayebati Postdoctoral Fellow co-advised by Tommi Jaakola. He will be in the group before starting a faculty position at JHU next year. 
- Shih-Peng Huang has returned to the group, now as a Master's student.
